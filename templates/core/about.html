OUR STORY

Building Footballers Since 2019

Venom Sports Football Academy was started in June 2019 with a vision to provide professional football training and create a strong pathway for young players.

We began our journey with just 2 coaches and 7 players. Within the first 2 months, our strength grew to 27 players, and within 7 months, we reached 80 players.

Today, Venom Sports Football Academy has grown into a strong football community with multiple training centres and players participating in competitive football across different levels.

PROFESSIONAL COACHING

Developing Players The Right Way

Our academy provides professional football training for players aged 4 to 18 years, guided by AIFF D & C Licensed Coaches.

Our aim is not only to teach children how to play football, but to develop confident, disciplined and competitive footballers who can progress to higher levels of the game.
